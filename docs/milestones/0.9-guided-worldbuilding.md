# Milestone 0.9 — Guided worldbuilding

## Goal

Make Ministry of Elsewhere understandable to a writer who has never used a
worldbuilding application. Writing remains the center of gravity. The app helps a
world emerge from prose, teaches only when a concept becomes relevant, and keeps
every durable creative decision explicit and reversible.

Release-readiness work is paused, not abandoned, while this experience is exercised.

## Product model

The daily rhythm has three distinct moments:

1. **Arrive** at World Home and choose the smallest useful next action.
2. **Write** in an ordinary draft, remembering or planning details when they become useful.
3. **Close the desk** after a safe save, optionally leaving a private note for next time.

Getting Started remains a separate repeatable learning guide. It is neither a
required checklist nor the closing ritual.

## Slices

### 0.9.1 — Daily rhythm foundation

- Opening a project shows World Home while retaining the restored active file and recovery state.
- World Home can open today's lazy-created Daily draft in one action.
- A restored document can be resumed without reloading or losing its selection-independent state.
- World Home gives world-project writers direct routes to lore creation and manuscript planning.
- Help is visibly separate and may be reopened at any time.
- Close the desk invokes the existing pending-save/conflict guard before showing completion state.
- An optional bounded note for next time is stored app-locally by project identity and never changes a project file.
- The closing screen does not intercept or replace operating-system Quit.

### 0.9.2 — Remember from writing

- A deliberate editor selection can begin a “Remember…” action.
- The writer chooses what the detail represents; the app does not classify prose silently.
- The exact selected words and destination are previewed before any file changes.
- Creating or connecting a note preserves the source prose and uses guarded, no-clobber writes.
- Cancel, stale source, ambiguity, and collision leave all project files unchanged.

### 0.9.3 — Guided lore creation

- Plain-language prompts lead with the writer's intent rather than storage templates.
- Character guidance begins with role, desire, difficulty, consistency, and connections; every field is optional.
- Place, event, faction, technology, species, spacecraft, and route guidance stays equally concise.
- Advanced frontmatter and continuity facts remain available without becoming prerequisites.
- Created notes remain ordinary portable Markdown and use the existing stable note identity.

### 0.9.4 — Guided timeline

- A first-use path begins with story start, one important prior event, and one approaching deadline.
- Writers may skip any anchor and proceed with partial or fictional dates.
- Every timeline item remains source-linked and writer-authored.
- The existing advanced timeline remains reachable without duplicating portable data.

### 0.9.5 — Contextual teaching

- Empty and first-use states explain the next useful action in one or two sentences.
- Guidance can be dismissed without pretending the feature was permanently learned.
- The complete learning guide remains reopenable through Help and Writing tools.
- Explanations distinguish lore, manuscript planning, Daily drafts, timelines, and continuity without jargon-first copy.

### 0.9.6 — Personal goals and challenges

- The daily word target remains a personal rhythm with 200 as the default.
- A challenge has its own name, target, `validFrom`, and `validTo`; the final valid day is inclusive.
- Future challenge dates show potential participation without fabricating progress.
- Missing a day never erases prior work or turns encouragement into shame.

## AI boundary

No AI is required for this milestone. A later decision gate may consider optional
assistance for extracting candidate details or reviewing unfiled Daily material only
after the deterministic workflow is useful by itself. That gate must separately
cover consent, keys, cost, network disclosure, retention, and a review-before-write
model. Manuscript text is never transmitted by default.

## Definition of done

- A new writer can explain where to write, what lore is, when to create it, and how
  Daily material can eventually join the manuscript or world bible.
- Common paths are keyboard and screen-reader operable and remain usable at enlarged zoom.
- No guidance makes hidden edits, awards mechanical daily credit, or weakens existing save/recovery behavior.
- App-local ritual state and portable project content are clearly distinguished in product copy and backup documentation.
- Focused automated checks, complete frontend checks, production build, and packaged macOS QA pass.
