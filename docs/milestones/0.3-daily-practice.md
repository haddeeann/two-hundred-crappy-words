# Milestone 0.3 — Daily practice

## Outcome

The application delivers its central promise: a writer can see, reach, and revisit a 200-word daily goal without the feature becoming punitive or fragile.

## Planned slices

### Word semantics

- Define Unicode-aware word tokenization with fixtures for punctuation, contractions, em dashes, numbers, and representative non-English text.
- Track document word count separately from daily credited words.
- Begin with the daily-credit behavior recorded in decision D-005.

### Daily ledger

- Persist progress per project and local calendar date.
- Record enough information to avoid double-credit after restart without storing manuscript text in the ledger.
- Handle midnight, time-zone changes, clock correction, and interrupted writes predictably.
- Keep the ledger human-inspectable or provide a documented export.

### Writing surface

- Show document count and today's `current / target` unobtrusively.
- Add a progress treatment that remains legible at different sizes and zoom levels.
- Provide a quiet, accessible completion moment once per day.
- Do not interrupt typing when the target is reached.

### Goals and history

- Keep 200 as the default while allowing a per-project or global target.
- Add a calendar/list history with daily credited words.
- Show current and best streaks without zeroing or shaming language.
- Allow correction of a mistaken daily entry with an audit-friendly local change.

## Out of scope

- Competitive leaderboards
- Social sharing
- Rewards that gate writing functionality
- Cloud accounts

## Milestone exit criteria

- Counts are deterministic and tested.
- Daily progress survives restart and date boundaries.
- The complete 200-word flow is manually demonstrated in the desktop app.
- Accessibility and reduced-motion behavior are verified.
