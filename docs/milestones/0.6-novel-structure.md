# Milestone 0.6 — Novel structure

## Outcome

A long manuscript can be composed as scenes and chapters, viewed at multiple levels, safely reordered, and compiled without trapping prose in a proprietary format.

## Planned slices

### Manuscript model

- Define portable chapter/scene ordering and stable identities.
- Store prose in Markdown files and keep structural metadata minimal.
- Add synopsis, point of view, location, story date, status, labels, and notes.
- Detect external moves or edits without silently dropping structure.

### Outline and corkboard

- Show the manuscript as an expandable outline.
- Provide a synopsis-focused corkboard.
- Reorder scenes and chapters with keyboard and pointer input.
- Preview the exact structural change and make it undoable.

### Writing views

- Show scene, chapter, selection, and manuscript counts.
- Add optional scene/chapter targets.
- Offer manuscript plus reference and distraction-reduced focus layouts.
- Support safe scene split and merge operations.

### Compile and export

- Compile ordered prose to Markdown and plain text first.
- Add DOCX and PDF only after style, metadata, and page-break rules are specified.
- Keep export separate from source files and never overwrite them implicitly.
- Produce an export report for omitted, missing, or ambiguous content.

## Decision gates

- Approve the stable manuscript ordering and metadata format.
- Approve representative corkboard and writing-layout direction.
- Approve DOCX/PDF formatting expectations before implementing final exporters.

The first gate is specified in [`../MANUSCRIPT_FORMAT.md`](../MANUSCRIPT_FORMAT.md) and was approved on 2026-08-22.

The corkboard direction was approved on 2026-08-25. The first writing-layout direction reuses the connected-lore reference pane for verified manuscript sources and treats Focus as ephemeral presentation state; it was adopted for prototype usability testing on 2026-08-26 and is recorded in D-028.

## Milestone exit criteria

- A representative multi-chapter manuscript can be reordered and compiled without content loss.
- Outline and corkboard operations are keyboard accessible.
- Exported order and content match the project structure through automated fixtures.
- A verified saved scene can split at an exact caret boundary through a three-path rollback-safe transaction, preserving the original identity and metadata on the left and offering guarded exact Undo.
- Two eligible adjacent verified scenes can merge through a four-path rollback-safe transaction, retaining the left identity and metadata, visibly retiring the exact right source, and offering guarded exact Undo.
- The persistent left rail remains a conventional project file tree; optional lore, manuscript, project, and practice context opens in a right-side writing-tools dock.
